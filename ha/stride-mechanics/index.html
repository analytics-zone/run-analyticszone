Stride Makanikai

The Biomechanics na Gudun Gudu

Asalin Ma'auni na Gudun Gudu

Ma'aunin Wuta

Gudu = Cadence (SPM) × Tsawon Tsayi

Fassara:Yadda kuke gudu ya dogara da yaddasau da yawaƘafafunku sun buga ƙasa (Cadence) suna ninka ta yayanisakuna tafiya tare da kowane cikakken tafiya (Tsawon Tafiya).

Wannan ma'auni na asali yana sarrafa duk ayyukan da ke gudana. Don samun sauri, dole ne ku ko dai:

  • Ƙara Cadence(juyawa da sauri) yayin kiyaye Tsawon Tsawon Hanya
  • Ƙara Tsawon Tsawon Tafiya(tafiya zuwa gaba da tafiya) yayin kiyaye Cadence
  • Inganta duka biyun(mafi kyawun tsarin aiki don manyan ayyuka)

⚖️ Tashin hankali

Cadence da Tsawon Tsayin Gabaɗayasabanin alaka. Yayin da ɗaya ya ƙaru, ɗayan yana son rage. Fasahar guje-guje ita ce gano ma'auni mafi kyau don taron ku, nau'in jiki, da na yanzu matakin dacewa.

Cadence (Mataki a Minti)

Menene Cadence?

Cadence, auna aMatakai a cikin Minti (SPM), shine jimlar adadin sau da ƙafafu suna bugun ƙasa a cikin minti ɗaya. Yana ɗaya daga cikin ma'auni mafi mahimmanci ga duka biyun gudun da kuma rigakafin rauni.

Formula

SPM = Jimlar Matakai / Tsawon lokaci (minti)

Misali:

Idan kun ɗauki matakai 180 a cikin tazarar daƙiƙa 60:

Cadence = 180/1 =180 SPM

📝 Stride vs. Mataki

A mafi yawan nazarin gudu,Cadenceyana nufin matakai (kowane bugun ƙafa). ATafiyaa zahiri yana wakiltar cikakken zagayowar (hagu + dama), don haka matakai 180 = 90 matakai.

Mahimman Mahimman Bayanan Cadence

Sauƙi / Run Run

Kwarewa:165-175 SPM
Mafari:150-165 SPM

Ƙaddamarwa / Marathon Pace

Manyan:175-185 SPM
Gasa:170-180 SPM

Gudu (400m - 1500m)

Elite:190-210 SPM
Gasa:180-195 SPM

🎯 Labarin ''180 SPM''

A tarihi, an dauki 180 SPM a matsayin "lambar sihiri." Binciken zamani ya nuna cewa yayin da manyan mutane Masu gudu suna tsayawa kusa ko sama da wannan alamar, mafi kyawun ƙwaƙƙwaran mutum ne bisa kafa tsayi da gudu.

Tafsirin Cadence

🐢 Cadence Too Low

Halaye:

  • Overstriding (ƙafar ƙafa a gaban tsakiyar taro)
  • Mahimmin billa a tsaye (high a tsaye oscillation)
  • Tasirin saukowa mai nauyi da haɗarin rauni mafi girma

Sakamako:Ƙarfin birki—kowane mataki yana rage ku kafin ku sake turawa gaba.

Gyara:Ƙara mitar mataki, mayar da hankali kan "ƙafafu masu sauri," ƙasa tare da ƙafa a ƙarƙashin kwatangwalo.

🏃 Cadence Too High

Halaye:

  • Choppy, gajeriyar matakai
  • Rashin isasshen tsawo da iko
  • Babban buƙatun bugun jini don ƙananan sauri

Sakamako:Talauci na tattalin arziki - kuna aiki tuƙuru amma ba ku yin tafiya mai nisa kowane mataki.

Gyara:Ayyukan wutar lantarki da aka yi niyya (tsaunuka), mai da hankali kan cikakken zagayowar tafiya da turawa.

⚡ Mafi kyawun Cadence

Halaye:

  • Ruwa, ingantaccen motsi
  • Karamin oscillation na tsaye
  • Lokacin tuntuɓar ƙasa mai sauri
  • Tsawa mai dorewa

Sakamako:Matsakaicin saurin tare da tasiri mai sarrafawa da kashe kuzari.

Yadda ake Nemo:Yi amfani da metronome ko kiɗa a 5-10% sama da ƙimar ku na asali.

Tsawon Tafiya

Menene Tsawon Tsawon Hanya?

Tsawon Tafiyaita ce tazarar da aka yi tafiya cikin cikakken zagayowar (mataki biyu). Yana wakiltar da ƙarfi da ingantattun injin tafiyar tafiyarku.

Formula

Tsawon Tsayi (m) = Nisa / (Jimlar Matakai / 2)

Ko:

Tsawon Tsayi = Gudu / (Cadence / 120)

Misali:

Gudun kilomita (1000m) cikin matakai 800:

Matakai/2 = Matakai 400
Tsawon Tafiya = 1000/400 =2.50m

Alamomin Tsawon Tsawon Tsawon Hankali

’Yan gudun hijira na qwarai (2:05 Marathon)

Tsawon Tafiya:1.8-2.1 mita

Masu Gasa

Tsawon Tafiya:1.4-1.7 mita

Masu Gudun Jiki

Tsawon Tafiya:1.0-1.3 mita

Masu farawa

Tsawon Tafiya:<1.0 mita

📏 Factor na Tsawo

Masu tsere masu tsayi a zahiri suna da tsayin daka na iya samun ci gaba, amma kuma suna da ƙari taro don motsawa.Tsawon Tsawon Dangi(Tsawon Tsayi / Tsawo) galibi yana da kyau nuna inganci fiye da cikakken nisa.

Abubuwan Da Suka Shafi Tsawon Tsawon Tafiya

1️⃣ Lokacin Tuntuɓar ƙasa (GCT)

Lokacin da ƙafarka ke ciyarwa a ƙasa. Gajeren GCT yana da alaƙa da mafi girman fitarwar wuta da ingantacciyar tafiyar da tattalin arziki.

Direba:Tsalle Plyometric, A-Skips, iyakoki.

2️⃣ Hip Extension & Power

Komawa baya ta cikin kugu a ƙarshen lokacin tsayawa. Cikakken tsawo yana fassara zuwa ƙari ciyar da gaba.

Direba:Hill maimaita, glute kunna motsa jiki, lunges.

3️⃣ Juyin Juya Tsaye

Rashin makamashi yana motsawa sama da ƙasa maimakon gaba. Yawan billa yana rage nisan tafiya da kalori ciyar.

Direba:Gudu tare da "ƙananan rufi" mayar da hankali, ainihin aikin kwanciyar hankali.

4️⃣ Taurin Kafa

Yadda kyaun jijiyoyi da tsokoki suke aiki azaman bazara. Ƙarfafa mafi girma yana ba da damar ƙarin dawowar makamashi da kowane mataki.

Direba:Yin tsalle-tsalle na igiya, pogo hops.

5️⃣ Rikicin Hannu

Hannun suna daidaita jujjuyawar kwatangwalo kuma suna taimakawa samar da tuƙi. Ƙananan injiniyoyi na hannu na iya ragewa tafiyar iko.

Direba:Wurin zama hannun yana gudana, annashuwa a kafaɗa.

Cadence (SPM) × Ma'auni Tsawon Tsayi

Masu tseren Elite ba kawai suna da babban Cadence ko tsayin tsayin tsayi ba - suna da mafi kyaun.hadedomin taron su.

Misalin Duniya na Gaskiya: Eliud Kipchoge (Marathon WR)

Ma'aunin Aiki:

  • Cadence (SPM): ~ 185 matakai/min
  • Tsayin Tafiya: ~ 1.90m
  • Gudun gudu: ~5.85m/s (2:52 min/km taki)

Bincike:Kipchoge yana kula da tsayin tsayi na musamman na sama da sa'o'i biyu. Nasa inganci ya fito ne daga ikon zama a 185 SPM yayin rufe kusan mita 2 tare da kowane cika zagayowar tafiya (hagu + matakan dama).

Binciken Halittu

🔴 Ƙarfafawa (Low Cadence)

Misali:Tsayin 2.2m × 150 SPM = 2.75 m/s

Matsala:Saukowa mai nisa a gaban tsakiyar taro yana haifar da ƙarfin birki da yana ƙara haɗarin rauni. Rashin inganci duk da tsayin daka.

🔴 Stride "Choppy" (Low Stride Length)

Misali:Tsayin 1.1m × 190 SPM = 1.7 m/s

Matsala:Babban farashin makamashi tare da ƙarancin motsawa. Yana jin aiki amma ba shi da ɗaukar hoto. Wuya don dorewa na dogon nisa.

🟢 Ingantaccen Tafiya (Maidace)

Misali:Tsayin 1.6m × 175 SPM = 2.3 m/s

Matsala:Wurin dadi inda aikin injiniya ya hadu da jijiyoyin jini dorewa.

✅ Nemo Madaidaicin Ma'auni

Saita:5 × 1km @ Takin kofa

  • Wakili #1:Gudu ta dabi'a, yi rikodin Cadence na asali da Tsawon Tsayinku
  • Wakili #2:Ƙara Cadence ta 5 SPM (gajerun matakai, matakai masu sauri), yi ƙoƙarin kiyaye taki
  • Wakili #3:Mayar da hankali kan tsayin hantsi mai ƙarfi (tsawon tsayi), ƙoƙarin kiyaye taki
  • Wakili #4:Koma zuwa tushen tushen, mayar da hankali kan matakan "springy".
  • Wakili #5:Lock a kan rhythm wanda ya fi dacewa (mafi ƙarancin RPE)

Maimaitawar da aka ji mafi sauƙi a saurin niyya yana wakiltar ma'aunin ku na yanzu mafi kyau.

Fihirisar Tafiya: Ma'aunin Ƙarfin Ƙarfi

Formula

Fihirisar Tafiya (SI) = Gudun gudu (m/s) × Tsawon Tsayin (m)

Stride Index yana haɗa sauri da inganci cikin awo ɗaya. Mafi girma SI gabaɗaya yana nuna mafi girma iya aiki.

Misali:

Gudun Gudu A: 5.0 m/s gudun × 1.6 m Tsawon Tsayin =SI ta 8.0
Gudun Gudun B: Gudun 5.0m/s × 1.8m Tsawon Tsawon Tsari =SI ta 9.0

Bincike:Mai Runner B yana rufe ƙarin ƙasa a kowane mataki a gudu ɗaya, yana nuna mafi kyau inji inganci.

🔬 Gidauniyar Bincike

Nazarin ya gano cewa ** haɓaka haɓaka ta 10% *** yana rage nauyi akan gwiwa da hip haɗin gwiwa, yana taimakawa wajen hana raunin da ya faru na kowa kamar gwiwa mai gudu. Yayin da ƙwararrun masu gudu suna matsakaicin 180+ SPM, mayar da hankali kan nemo rhythm ɗin da ke ba ka damar saukar da ƙafarka a ƙarƙashin kwatangwalo (yajin tsaka tsaki) maimakon fiye da bin kafaffen lamba.

Makullin shine ** inganci ***: haɓaka fitarwar wutar lantarki yayin da rage farashin injinan motsi.

Aikace-aikacen Horarwa Na Aiki

🎯 Saitin Kula da Cadence

Tsawon Mita 1000

Yi amfani da metronome ko kiɗan BPM

  1. 1km: Baseline cadence (gudu ta dabi'a)
  2. 1km: Cadence +5 SPM (gajerun matakai masu sauri)
  3. 1km: Komawa zuwa matakin asali
  4. 1km: Cadence +10 SPM (mayar da hankali kan juyawa)

Manufar:Haɓaka ikon jijiya don canza juzu'i cikin sauri daban-daban.

🎯 Saitin Ƙarfin Tsayin Tsawon Tsayin

Maimaita Tudun (6-8 × 200m)

Mayar da hankali kan haɓakar kwatangwalo mai ƙarfi

  1. Kora gwiwoyinku gaba da girma
  2. Turawa da ƙarfi daga ƙasa
  3. Tsaya tsayin tsayi

Manufar:Gina ƙarfin fashewar da ake buƙata don tsawaita tafiyarku ba tare da wuce gona da iri.

Jagoran Makanikai, Jagoran Gudu

Gudu = Cadence × Tsawon Tsayi ba dabara ba ce kawai - tsari ne na fahimta da inganta kowane fanni na fasaha na gudu.

Bibiya duka masu canji. Gwaji tare da ma'auni. Nemo mafi kyawun haɗin ku. Gudu zai biyo baya.
