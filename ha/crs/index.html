Menene Gudun Gudun Mahimmanci?

Gudun gudu mai mahimmanci (CRS)shine matsakaicin matsakaicin saurin gudu da za ku iya kula ba tare da gajiyawa ba. Yana wakiltar mashigin motsin motsin ku, yawanci daidai zuwa 4 mmol/L lactate na jini kuma yana dawwama na kusan mintuna 30-60. Ana ƙididdige CRS ta amfani da biyu gwaje-gwaje mafi girma na lokaci (misali, 1200m da 3600m) don ƙayyade wuraren horo na keɓaɓɓen don taki. ingantawa.

Matsakaicin saurin gudu yana wakiltar matsakaicin iyakar gudu da zaku iya kiyayewa ci gaba ba tare da gajiyawa ba. Ƙofar ku ta motsa jiki-ƙarfin inda samar da lactate yayi daidai rashin lactate.

🎯 Muhimmancin Jiki

CRS yayi daidai da:

  • Ƙarfin Lactate 2 (LT2)- Ƙofar iska ta biyu
  • Mafi Girman Lactate Steady State (MLSS)- Mafi ɗorewa matakin lactate
  • Matsakaicin Ƙaddamar aiki (FTP)- Gudu daidai da hawan keke FTP
  • ~ 4 mmol/L lactate na jini- Alamar OBLA ta al'ada

Me yasa CRS Mahimmanci

Gudun gudu mai mahimmanci shinetushen awowanda ke buɗe duk nauyin horo na ci gaba bincike:

  • Yankunan horo:Yana keɓance yankuna masu ƙarfi dangane da ilimin halittar ku
  • rTSS Lissafi:Yana ba da damar ingantaccen horo Ƙididdigar Makin Danniya
  • CTL/ATL/TSB:Da ake buƙata don Aiwatarwa Ma'aunin Ma'auni na Gudanarwa
  • Bibiyar Ci gaba:Ma'aunin ma'aunin haɓakar motsa jiki na motsa jiki
⚠️ Mahimman Dogara:Ba tare da ingantaccen gwajin CRS ba, ma'aunin nauyin horo na ci gaba (rTSS, CTL, ATL, TSB) ba za a iya ƙididdige su ba. Wani kuskuren CRS zai lalata duk binciken horo na gaba.

Me yasa Zabi CRS?

Ba kamar VO₂max (yana buƙatar gwajin lab) ko ƙofar lactate (yana buƙatar samfuran jini), gudana mai mahimmanci Ana iya auna gudu akan kowace waƙa tare da agogon gudu kawai. Yana bayar da fahimtar horo iri ɗaya kamar Gwajin gwaje-gwaje masu tsada yayin kasancewa masu amfani don sake gwadawa akai-akai kowane mako 6-8. Koyi game da dukagudanar da hanyoyin gwajin aikida lokacin da za a amfani da kowane.

Protocol na Gwajin CRS

📋 Standard Protocol

  1. Dumi-dumi

    Mintuna 15-20 cikin sauƙin gudu, rawar jiki mai ƙarfi, da matakan 4-6 don shirya don babban ƙarfi kokarin.

  2. Gwajin Lokaci 1200m

    Matsakaicin yunƙurin ƙoƙarce-ƙoƙarce (hanyoyi 3 akan hanya mai tsayin mita 400). Yi rikodin jimlar lokacin. Manufar: mafi sauri mai yiwuwa matsakaicin taki.

  3. Cikakken farfadowa

    Minti 15-30na saurin gudu ko tafiya/hutawa. Wannan damar don kawar da lactate da dawo da aerobic.

  4. 3600m Gwajin Lokaci

    Matsakaicin ƙoƙarin ƙoƙarin (lafi 9 akan hanya mai tsayin mita 400). Ci gaba da tafiya. Ana iya yin wannan a kan a rana daban idan an buƙata.

CRS Formula na Lissafi

Formula

CRS (m/s) = (D₂ - D₁) / (T₂ - T₁)

Inda:

  • D₁ = Gajeren nisa (misali, mita 1200)
  • D₂ = Mafi tsayi (misali, mita 3600)
  • T₁ = Lokacin D₁ (a cikin dakika)
  • T₂ = Lokacin D₂ (a cikin dakika)

🔬 Me yasa Gudun Gudu Mai Muhimmanci ke Aiki

Gudun gudu mai mahimmanci yana wakiltar iyaka tsakaninmotsa jiki mai nauyi da tsanani yankuna. A ƙasa CRS, samar da lactate da sharewa sun kasance daidai-zaku iya gudu don tsawaita lokaci. Sama da CRS, lactate yana taruwa a hankali har sai ya gaji a cikin mintuna 20-40.

Wannan yana sa CRS cikakkiyar ƙarfi don:

  • Saita matakan tsere masu ɗorewa don 5K zuwa abubuwan marathon
  • Maganihoro tazara kofa
  • Kula da haɓakar motsa jiki na motsa jiki akan lokaci
  • Lissafinauyin horo da buƙatun farfadowa

Yadda ake Gwada Gudun Gudun Mahimmancinku

Mataki 1: Dumi Da kyau

Cika mintuna 15-20 na saurin gudu, tsayin daka mai ƙarfi, da matakai 4-6. Wannan yana shirya tsokoki da tsarin zuciya da jijiyoyin jini don iyakar ƙoƙari.

Mataki na 2: Yi Gwajin Lokaci 1200m

Gudun mita 1200 (laps 3 akan hanya mai nisan mita 400) a iyakar ƙoƙarin da aka ɗora. Yi rikodin lokacin ku zuwa na biyu. Mayar da hankali akan kiyaye daidaito, babban ƙarfi a cikin TT.

Mataki na 3: Farfadowa Gabaɗaya

Huta don minti 15-30. Kuna iya tafiya ko yin gudu a hankali. Yana da mahimmanci don ƙyale zuciyar ku raguwar ƙimar kuma ƙafafunku sun warke sosai kafin ƙoƙari na gaba.

Mataki na 4: Yi Gwajin Lokaci 3600m

Gudu mita 3600 (laps 9 akan hanya mai nisan mita 400) a iyakar ƙoƙari. Wannan shi ne bangaren jimiri na gwajin. Tsaya mafi saurin gudu da za ku iya ɗauka don tsayin daka.

Mataki na 5: Lissafin CRS

Yi amfani da dabarar linzamin kwamfuta: (D2 - D1) / (T2 - T1). Misali: (3600 - 1200) / (864s - 252s) = 2400 / 612 = 3.92 m/s. Juya zuwa taki: 100 / 3.92 = 25.5s/100m.

Aikace-aikace masu amfani na Gudun Gudun Mahimmanci

1️⃣ Buɗe Ma'aunin Load na Horo

Gudun gudu mai mahimmanci shine ƙididdigewa a cikin ƙididdige Factor Factor donrTSS (Gudun Makin Damuwar Koyarwa). Ba tare da shi ba, ku ba zai iya ƙididdige damuwa na motsa jiki ko bin yanayin dacewa / gajiyarwa ba.

2️⃣ Keɓance Yankunan Horowa

Jadawalin taki na yau da kullun ba su da lissafin ilimin ilimin halittar mutum ɗaya.Yankunan horo na tushen CRStabbatar da kowane mai gudu yayi horo a mafi kyawun ƙarfin su don gudanawar ƙofar aerobic.

3️⃣ Kula da Ci gaban Lafiya

A sake gwadawa kowane mako 6-8. Haɓaka saurin gudu mai mahimmanci (mafi sauri) yana nuna nasara aerobic daidaitawa. Stagnant CRS yana ba da shawarar horarwa yana buƙatar daidaitawa.

4️⃣ Hasashen Ayyukan tsere

Takin CRS yana ƙayyadad da dorewar madaidaicin matakin. Yi amfani da shi don saita haƙiƙanin manufa don 5K, 10K, da abubuwan da suka faru na rabin marathon.

5️⃣ Zane-zanen Ma'auni

Classic CRS saitin: 10×400 @ CRS taki (sauran 60s), 4×1200 @ 98% CRS (saura 90s). Gina izinin lactate iya aiki.

6️⃣ Inganta Dabarun Taper

Bi CRS kafin da bayan taper. Mai nasara taper yana kula ko dan inganta CRS yayin rage gajiya (ƙara TSB).

Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi Game da Mahimman Gudun Gudu

Menene saurin gudu mai mahimmanci (CRS)?

Matsakaicin saurin gudu shine matsakaicin saurin gudu wanda zaku iya ɗauka na kusan 30-60 mintuna ba tare da tara gajiya ba. Yana wakiltar bakin kofa na motsin motsa jiki - wurin da Samuwar lactate daidai yake da izinin lactate (yawanci 4 mmol / L na lactate na jini). CRS ne ƙididdiga daga gwaje-gwajen lokaci na max-kokari kuma yana aiki azaman tushe don keɓantacceyankunan horokumaTSS lissafi.

Ta yaya zan lissafta CRS na?

Don ƙididdige saurin gudu mai mahimmanci: (1) dumama yadda ya kamata, (2) Yi iyakar ƙoƙari Gwajin lokacin 1200m, (3) Huta na mintuna 15-30, (4) Yi iyakar ƙoƙarin gwaji na 3600m, (5) Yi amfani da dabarar:CRS (m/s) = (D2 - D1) / (T2 - T1). Maida sakamakon don gudun mita 100 ko kowace km. Yi amfani da kalkuleta na CRS kyauta a sama don sakamako nan take.

Sau nawa zan gwada CRS na?

Sake gwada saurin gudu mai mahimmanci kowane mako 6-8 don sabunta yankunan horonku azaman naku dacewa yana inganta. Gwaje-gwaje akai-akai (kowane makonni 4) na iya zama dacewa yayin tsananin tubalan horo, yayin da ƙarancin gwaji (makonni 10-12) ke aiki don matakan kulawa. Gudun ƙofa ɗinku yakamata ya inganta ci gaba tare da ingantaccen horo, yana nuna tabbataccen daidaitawar aerobic.

Shin CRS daidai yake da ƙofar lactate ko FTP?

CRS yayi kama da juna amma ba iri ɗaya bane. Gudun gudu mai mahimmanci yayi daidai da na biyun ku Ƙofar lactate (LT2, kusa da 4 mmol/L), wanda ya ɗan fi girma fiye da lactate na farko. bakin kofa (LT1, 2 mmol/L). Yana da kwatankwacinsa da Tafin Ƙaƙwalwar Ƙarfi (FTP), amma FTP shine dangane da ƙoƙari na mintuna 60 yayin da CRS ke ƙididdige shi daga gwajin ɗan gajeren lokaci. CRS yawanci yana wakiltar taki mai dorewa na mintuna 30-40, yana mai da shi manufa don gudun tseren 5K shiri da horon kofa.

Menene kyakkyawan CRS don masu farawa?

Don masu tasowa masu tasowa, CRS na yau da kullun yana sama da 31s a kowace 100m (> 5:10 min/km). Masu tseren motsa jiki yawanci cimma 25-31s a cikin 100m (4:10-5:10 min/km). Kada ku kwatanta kanku da manyan mutane ’yan wasa — mayar da hankali kan inganta naku CRS ta hanyar ingantaccen horo. Duk wani CRS yana aiki wurin farawa don inganta taki.

Zan iya amfani da CRS don horon marathon?

Ee, amma tare da gyare-gyare. CRS yana wakiltar saurin da za ku iya ɗauka na mintuna 30-40, wanda shine mai tsanani sosai don cikakken marathon. Koyaya, yankunan horo na tushen CRS suna da kyau ga marathon Shirye-shiryen: Yanki 2 yana gina tushen iska, Yanki 3 yana haɓaka ƙayyadaddun juriya, kuma Yanki 4 (a CRS) yana inganta madaidaicin lactate. Takin Marathon yawanci yana faɗuwa 105-110% na tafiyar CRS ɗin ku (a hankali fiye da bakin kofa). Yi amfani da CRS don tsara horon ku, ba kamar yadda gudun ku.

Me yasa farfadowa tsakanin gwaji na lokaci yana da mahimmanci?

Idan ba ku murmure sosai tsakanin gwaje-gwajen lokaci biyu ba, gajiya za ta yi jinkiri lokacinku na biyu, yin lissafin saurin gudu mai mahimmanci ya bayyana da sauri fiye da gaskiya. Wannan yana haifar da wuce gona da iri na horo. Tabbatar da aƙalla mintuna 15-30 na hutawa ko yi la'akari da yin gwaje-gwaje a ranaku daban-daban a cikin mako guda.

Zan iya kimanta CRS daga lokutan tsere maimakon gwaji?

Yayin da zaku iya kimanta CRS daga wasan tsere, gwajin kai tsaye ya fi daidai. Idan ka dole ne a kimanta: ɗauki mafi kyawun lokacin 10K na kwanan nan kuma ku yi amfani da wannan taki a cikin 100m debe ~ 1 seconds, ko Yi amfani da 103-105% na rabin gudun marathon ku. Koyaya, gwajin 1200m + 3600m yana ba da ainihin bayanan da ake buƙata don ingantaccen horo.
