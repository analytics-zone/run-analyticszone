Tattalin Arziki Gudu: Sirrin Gudu Da Sauri

Yadda ake amfani da ƙarancin kuzari a cikin taki ɗaya kuma buɗe yuwuwar ku ta gudu

Menene Tattalin Arziki Gudun?

Gudun Tattalin Arziki (RE)auna yadda jikinka ke amfani da iskar oxygen a wani taki da aka bayar. An bayyana shi azaman adadin iskar oxygen da ake cinyewa (ml/kg) a kowace kilomita guda. Ƙananan farashin iskar oxygen ɗin ku a ƙayyadaddun taki, mafi kyawun tattalin arzikin ku mai gudana-ma'ana zaku iya gudu da sauri ta amfani da adadin kuzari iri ɗaya.

Yi tunanin tafiyar da tattalin arziki kamar "mil per galan" na jikin ku. Masu gudu biyu masu iri ɗayaƘimar VO2maxna iya samun wasannin tsere daban-daban idan mutum yana da babban tattalin arziki. A gaskiya ma, tafiyar da tattalin arziki sau da yawa yana bambanta masu gudu masu kyau daga manyan, musamman a tseren marathon da abubuwan da suka faru na nesa.

Gudun Tattalin Arziki Mai Sauri:

  • Gwanayen marathon:150-170 ml/kg/km kudin oxygen
  • Ƙwararrun masu gudu:180-200 ml/kg/km
  • Masu tsere na nishaɗi:200-220 ml/kg/km
  • yuwuwar haɓakawa:4-8% a cikin watanni 6-12 tare da horar da hankali
  • Tasiri:5% haɓakar tattalin arziki = 5% lokutan tsere cikin sauri

Me yasa Gudun Tattalin Arziki Ya Fi Muhimmanci Fiye da VO2max

YayinVO2max yana samun ƙarin kulawa, Gudun tattalin arziki sau da yawa shine mafi kyawun hasashen aikin gudu na nisa, musamman ga gudun marathon da kuma bayansa.

Shaidar Bincike

Nazarin ƙwararrun masu tsere na nesa sun nuna cewa tattalin arziƙin gudu yana bayyana 65-80% na bambancin aiki a lokutan marathon, idan aka kwatanta da kawai 40-50% don VO2max. Wannan yana nufin cewa a tsakanin masu gudu masu irin wannan dabi'un VO2max, waɗanda ke da mafi kyawun tattalin arziƙi suna tafiya cikin sauri.

Shahararren misali:Derek Clayton ya kafa rikodin marathon na duniya a cikin 1969 (2:08:33) duk da ɗan ƙaramin VO2max na 69 ml/kg/min — ƙasa da yawancin fafatawa. Tattalin arzikinsa na musamman (wanda aka ƙiyasta a 150 ml/kg/km) ya ba shi damar canza ƙarfin motsa jiki zuwa lokutan tseren gudu.

Abubuwan Da Suka Shafi Tattalin Arzikin Gudu

Gudun tattalin arziƙin yana tasiri ta hanyar biomechanics, physiology, da horo. Fahimtar waɗannan abubuwan yana taimaka muku gano wuraren ingantawa.

1. Abubuwan Halittu

Lokacin Tuntuɓar ƙasa

Masu tseren gwanaye suna kashe ɗan lokaci a ƙasa tare da kowane mataki - yawanci mil 180-200 millise seconds idan aka kwatanta da 220-260ms don masu tseren nishaɗi. Gajeren lokacin tuntuɓar ƙasa yana rage ƙarfin birki kuma yana haɓaka ƙarfin dawo da ƙarfi daga tendons da tsokoki.

Auna lokacin hulɗar ƙasatare da agogon guje-guje na zamani yana taimaka wa bibiyar ingantattun ingantattun biomechanical.

Juyawa A tsaye

Yawan motsi sama da ƙasa yana lalata kuzari. Masu tseren fitattu galibi suna billa 6-8cm a kowane tafiya, yayin da masu tseren nishaɗi sukan wuce 10-12cm.Rage oscillation a tsayeta 1-2cm na iya inganta tattalin arziki da 2-3%.

Cadence

Yawancin ƙwararrun masu gudu suna kula da matakai 180+ a minti daya.Mafi kyawu mai saurin guduyana rage tasirin tasiri, yana rage wuce gona da iri, kuma yana haɓaka ma'ajin makamashi na roba. Ƙara yawan adadin ta 5-10% sau da yawa yana inganta tattalin arziki ga masu gudu tare da ƙananan ƙarancin (<170 spm).

Tsawon Tafiya

An inganta tattalin arziƙin gudu lokacin da tsayin tafiya ya dace da tsayin ƙafarku, motsin hip, da ƙarfi. Juyawa (saukarwa da ƙafa mai nisa a gaban tsakiyar taro) yana bata kuzari ta hanyar birki. Tsawon tsayin dabi'a a mafi kyawun ƙima yana ƙara haɓaka aiki.

2. Abubuwan Halitta

Haɗin Fiber Muscle

Slow-twitch (Nau'in I) zaruruwan tsoka sun fi tattalin arziƙi fiye da filaye masu sauri (Nau'in II). Masu tseren nesa na Elite yawanci suna da 70-80% sannu a hankali abun da ke ciki. Yayin da kwayoyin halitta ke ƙayyade nau'in fiber na asali, horo na iya inganta ƙarfin oxidative na filaye masu sauri.

Mitochondrial Density

Ƙarin mitochondria = mafi kyawun iskar oxygen da iskar oxygen. Doguwa, mai sauƙin shigaYanki 2ƙara yawan mitochondrial a cikin watanni da shekaru, inganta tattalin arziki ta hanyar haɓaka ƙona mai da adana glycogen.

Taurin Jiki

Stiffer Achilles tendons suna adanawa kuma suna dawo da makamashi mai ƙarfi da inganci. Horon Plyometric, tsaunuka sprints, da saurin gudu suna haɓaka taurin jijiyoyi, haɓaka tattalin arziki da kashi 2-4%. Wannan karbuwa yana ɗaukar makonni 8-12 na daidaiton horo.

Haɗin Jiki

Ɗaukar ƙarancin wuce haddi na jiki yana inganta tattalin arziki. Kowane 1kg rage nauyin jiki yana inganta tattalin arzikin tafiyar da kusan 1%. Duk da haka, rasa yawan ƙwayar tsoka na iya zama rashin amfani - mayar da hankali kan rage kitsen jiki yayin da yake riƙe da tsoka.

3. Abubuwan Horarwa

Girman horo

Mafi girman nisan mako-mako yana da alaƙa da ingantacciyar tattalin arziki, har zuwa aya. Yawancin bincike yana nuna haɓakar tattalin arziki tare da mil 50-70 a kowane mako, tare da raguwar dawowa sama da mil 80-90 ga masu tseren da ba fitattu ba. Daidaituwa yana da mahimmanci fiye da tsinkayar mako guda.

Rarraba Ƙarfin Horarwa

The80/20 tsarin horoyana inganta tattalin arziƙi ta hanyar haɓaka haɓakar motsa jiki yayin da yake ba da damar murmurewa daga zaman wahala. Matsakaicin ƙarfi mai yawa (Yanki na 3) yana yin illa ga ci gaban tattalin arziki.

Ingantaccen Neuromuscular

Tsarin ku na jijiya yana koya don ɗaukar tsokoki yadda ya kamata a cikin shekaru masu gudu. Wannan shine dalilin da ya sa ƙwararrun masu gudu sukan sami mafi kyawun tattalin arziki fiye da masu farawa, har ma da irin wannan VO2max. Daidaituwa da haƙuri suna gina ingantaccen neuromuscular.

Yadda Ake Auna Gudun Tattalin Arziki

Gwajin gwaje-gwaje (Gold Standard)

Hanyar da ta fi dacewa tana auna yawan iskar oxygen yayin aiki mai ƙarfi a cikin taki da yawa ta amfani da keken motsa jiki. Masu gudu suna kammala matakan mintuna 4-6 a cikin sauri cikin sauri yayin da suke sanye da abin rufe fuska wanda ke tantance iskar oxygen da carbon dioxide.

Sakamako ya nuna:

  • Farashin Oxygen (ml/kg/km) a matakai daban-daban
  • Yadda tattalin arziki ke canzawa da sauri
  • Kwatanta farashin makamashi zuwa gwaje-gwaje na asali

Farashin:$150-300 a kowane gwaji
Mitar:Gwada kowane watanni 3-6 don bin diddigin ingantawa

Kiyasin Tattalin Arzikin Fage

Duk da yake ƙasa da madaidaicin gwaje-gwajen lab, hanyoyin filin suna ba da alamun tattalin arziki masu amfani:

Yawan Zuciya a Kafaffen Taki

Idan bugun zuciyar ku ya ragu a wani taki na tsawon lokaci, tattalin arzikin ku yana inganta. Bibiyar ƙimar zuciya yayin gudanar da ɗan lokaci na yau da kullun ko ƙoƙarin ƙoƙarin sa ido kan canje-canjen tattalin arziki.

Misali:Idan bugun zuciyar ku a gudun marathon ya ragu daga 165 bpm zuwa 158 bpm sama da makonni 12, tattalin arzikin ku ya inganta da kusan 4-5%.

Taki a Kafaffen Matsayin Zuciya

Gudu a cikin bugun zuciya ɗaya (misali, 150 bpm) don gwaje-gwaje masu yawa. Idan saurin ku ya ƙaru yayin da bugun zuciya ke tsayawa, tattalin arzikin ku ya inganta.

Gudun Mahimmanci (CRS)

Gudu Mai Muhimmancia kaikaice yana nuna tafiyar da tattalin arziki. Haɓakawa a cikin CRS ba tare da madaidaicin VO2max yana nuna haɓakar tattalin arziki ba. Run Analytics yana bin CRS yana canzawa ta atomatik.

Ma'aunin Smartwatch

Agogon GPS na zamani suna ba da ma'auni masu alaƙa da tattalin arziki:

  • Lokacin tuntuɓar ƙasa:Manufar <220ms
  • Juyawa ta tsaye:Manufar <9cm
  • Cadence:Target 175-185 spm don yawancin masu gudu
  • Rabo a tsaye:Tsawon oscillation ÷ tsayin tafiya, manufa <8%

Bibiyar waɗannan ma'aunin tsawon makonni da watanni. Abubuwan haɓakawa a cikin injiniyoyin halittu galibi suna da alaƙa da ingantacciyar tattalin arziƙin gudu.

Yadda Ake Inganta Tattalin Arzikin Gudu

Gudun tattalin arziki yana da horo sosai. Anan akwai hanyoyin tushen shaida don ingantawa:

1. Babban Mileage Base Training

Madaidaicin ƙarar aerobic shine tushen tafiyar da tattalin arziki. Doguwa, mai sauƙin shigaYanki 2 (60-70% max HR)Ƙirƙirar daidaitawa waɗanda ke inganta tattalin arziki:

  • Ƙara yawan mitochondrial
  • Ingantaccen cibiyar sadarwa na capillary
  • Ingantaccen iskar shaka mai
  • Kyakkyawan daidaitawar neuromuscular

Shawarwari:Gina zuwa mil 40-60 a kowane mako (na nishaɗi), mil 60-80+ (gasa) sama da watanni 3-6. Amfanidace periodizationdon kauce wa wuce gona da iri.

2. Kofa da Tempo Gudu

Gudu amatakin lactate(Yanki na 4) na mintuna 20-40 yana inganta tattalin arziki ta hanyar koyar da jikin ku don share lactate da kyau kuma yana gudana cikin sauri da sauri.

Misalin motsa jiki:

  • Ci gaba na ɗan lokaci: Minti 30-40 a matakin ƙasa (da wuya)
  • Tazarar jirgin ruwa: 3-4 × 8-10 mintuna a bakin kofa tare da dawo da mintoci 2-3
  • Ci gaba na ɗan lokaci: Fara a gudun marathon, ci gaba zuwa rabin gudun marathon sama da mintuna 40

3. Saurin Gudu da Tafiya

Gajerun fashe na saurin gudu (80-100m yana tafiya a cikin 5K taki ko sauri) sau 2-3 a kowane mako yana haɓaka haɓakar neuromuscular da injina masu gudana ba tare da gajiya mai yawa ba.

Yadda ake ci gaba:

  • Bayan sauƙin gudu, yi 4-6 × 80-100m accelerations
  • Gina ƙoƙarin 95% akan 20m, riƙe don 60m, rage sama da 20m
  • Komawa baya don farawa don cikakken farfadowa tsakanin maimaitawa
  • Mayar da hankali kan annashuwa, tsari mai santsi-ba iyakar gudu ba

4. Horon Dutse

Hill yana maimaita haɓaka ƙarfin ƙafafu, ƙarfi, da taurin jijiyoyi-duk masu ba da gudummawa ga tafiyar da tattalin arziki. Hills yana tilasta mafi kyawun biomechanics: gajeriyar lokacin tuntuɓar ƙasa, mafi girman ƙima, rage wuce gona da iri.

Misalin motsa jiki na Hill:

  • Gajerun tudu:8-10 × 60-90 seconds a ƙoƙarin 5K, yi ƙasa don murmurewa
  • Dogayen tuddai:Mintuna 4-6 × 3-5 a ƙoƙarin ƙofa, saurin gudu
  • Gudun tsalle-tsalle:6-8 × 10-15 daƙiƙa mafi girman ƙoƙari akan tudu mai tsayi

5. Plyometrics da Ƙarfafa Horarwa

Ƙananan plyometrics na jiki suna inganta taurin jijiyoyi da dawowar kuzari. Bincike ya nuna zaman 2-3 a kowane mako na plyometrics na iya inganta tattalin arziki da 3-5% a cikin makonni 8-12.

Ayyuka masu inganci:

  • Akwatin tsalle: 3 × 10 maimaitawa
  • Hops guda ɗaya: 3 × 20 kowace kafa
  • Tsalle mai zurfi: 3 × 8 maimaitawa
  • Tsaya: 4-6 × 30m
  • Tsalle: 4-6 × 40m

Haɗa tare da horarwa mai ƙarfi (squats, deadlifts, lunges) don gina ƙarfin tsoka wanda ke goyan bayan ikon plyometric.

6. Inganta Form Gudu

Haɓaka aikin injiniyana iya samar da 2-5% ribar tattalin arziki:

  • Haɓakawa:Ƙara 5-10% idan ƙasa da 170 spm
  • Rage juzu'i a tsaye:Mayar da hankali kan motsi gaba, ba bouncing ba
  • Inganta matsayi:Rage gaba kadan daga idon sawu, ba kugu ba
  • Huta na sama:Tashin hankali a cikin kafadu da makamai yana lalata makamashi
  • Tsakanin ƙafar ƙafa:Ƙasa ƙarƙashin tsakiyar taro, ba diddige-farko daga gaba ba

Tsanaki:Kada ka tilasta canje-canjen tsari mai ban mamaki na dare ɗaya. gyare-gyare a hankali a cikin watanni yana ba da damar daidaitawa ba tare da rauni ba.

7. Rage nauyi (Idan Ya dace)

Ga masu gudu masu ɗauke da kitsen jiki mai yawa, rasa 2-5kg na iya inganta tattalin arziki da 2-5%. Duk da haka, kauce wa cin abinci mai tsanani a lokacin horo mai nauyi-wannan yana daidaita farfadowa kuma zai iya haifar da asarar tsoka.

Hanyar ɗorewa:Rasa 0.25-0.5kg a kowane mako ta hanyar ƙarancin kalori mai matsakaici yayin da ake ci gaba da cin abinci mai gina jiki da daidaiton horo.

Gudun Tattalin Arziki vs. Hadarin Rauni

Wasu hanyoyin inganta tattalin arziki suna ƙara haɗarin rauni idan an aiwatar da su da ƙarfi:

Dabarun Haɗari Mai Girma

  • Ƙaruwa cikin sauri:Bi ka'idar haɓaka 10% mako-mako
  • Matsakaicin plyometrics:Fara tare da zaman 2 a kowane mako, ƙananan ƙara
  • Ƙaruwa na tilastawa:Canza a hankali sama da makonni 8-12
  • Canjin takalma mafi ƙanƙanta:Canji a hankali sama da watanni 6+

Dabaru masu ƙarancin haɗari

  • Sauƙin ƙarar gudu:Mafi aminci mai gina tattalin arziki
  • Tafiya bayan saurin gudu:Ƙananan haɗarin rauni, babban dawowa
  • Ƙarfafa horo:Kariya daga rauni idan an yi daidai
  • Ci gaba a hankali:Hakuri yana hana rauni

Saka idanuhoro load awo awo(CTL/ATL/TSB) don tabbatar da horar da mai da hankali kan tattalin arziƙi bai wuce ƙarfin dawo da ku ba.

Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi Game da Tattalin Arziki Guda

Menene mafi mahimmanci: VO2max ko tafiyar da tattalin arziki?

Don gudun marathon da matsananci nisa, tafiyar da tattalin arziƙin yawanci yakan fi hasashen aiki. Masu gudu biyu masu kama da VO2max na iya samun 5-10% bambanci a lokutan marathon dangane da tattalin arziki kadai. Don guntun tsere (5K-10K), VO2max ya zama mafi mahimmanci.

Nawa tafiyar da tattalin arzikin zai iya inganta?

Yawancin masu gudu na iya inganta tattalin arziki ta hanyar 5-10% a cikin shekarar farko na horar da hankali, tare da ƙarin 3-5% mai yiwuwa a cikin shekaru masu zuwa. Elite masu gudu yawanci suna haɓaka 1-2% kowace shekara da zarar sun isa matakan aiki masu girma. Masu farawa sukan ga ribar mafi sauri (10-15% a cikin watanni 6-12).

Shin rasa nauyi yana inganta tattalin arzikin gudu?

Ee, asarar kitsen jiki da ya wuce kima yana inganta tattalin arziki da kusan 1% a kowace kilogiram da aka rasa. Duk da haka, asarar ƙwayar tsoka na iya zama rashin amfani. Mayar da hankali kan asarar mai mai ɗorewa (0.25-0.5kg a kowane mako) yayin kiyaye ƙarfi da daidaiton horo.

Shin canza tsarin gudu zai iya inganta tattalin arziki?

Ee, amma dole ne canje-canje su kasance a hankali. Haɓakawa ta hanyar 5-10% (idan a halin yanzu <170 spm), rage oscillation a tsaye, da haɓaka matsayi na iya samar da 2-5% tattalin arzikin tattalin arziki sama da makonni 8-16. Tilastawa, canje-canje masu ban mamaki sukan haifar da rauni.

Shin ƙananan takalma suna inganta tattalin arzikin gudu?

Bincike ya haɗu. Wasu nazarin suna nuna haɓakar tattalin arziki na 2-4% a cikin ƙwararrun ƙwararrun masu amfani da takalma, yayin da wasu ba su nuna wani fa'ida ko ƙara haɗarin rauni a lokacin canji. Idan canzawa, yi haka a hankali sama da watanni 6-12 tare da ƙarancin nisan tafiya da farko.

Ta yaya plyometrics ke inganta tattalin arziƙin gudu?

Plyometrics yana ƙara ƙanƙarar jijiyoyi da haɓaka ajiyar makamashi na roba da dawowa. Wannan yana nufin tendons ɗinku suna aiki kamar maɓuɓɓugan ruwa, suna adana kuzari akan saukowa da sakewa yayin turawa. Bincike ya nuna 3-5% haɓakar tattalin arziki daga makonni 8-12 na daidaitaccen horo na plyometric.

Shin tafiyar da tattalin arzikin halitta ce?

Genetics suna lissafin kusan kashi 40-50% na tattalin arzikin tafiyar da tushe (nau'in fiber na tsoka, kaddarorin tendon, adadin jiki). Koyaya, horo yana lissafin sauran 50-60%, ma'ana ingantaccen haɓakawa yana yiwuwa ba tare da la’akari da farkon asalin halitta ba.

Shin tafiyar da tattalin arzikin yana raguwa da shekaru?

Ee, amma ƙasa da VO2max. Tattalin arziki yana raguwa kusan 1-2% a kowace shekara goma bayan shekaru 40, idan aka kwatanta da raguwar 5-10% a VO2max. Wannan shine dalili guda daya da yasa masu tseren masters zasu iya kasancewa masu gasa - kiyaye tattalin arziki wani bangare na raguwar VO2max.

Har yaushe ake ɗauka don ganin an inganta tattalin arziki?

Daidaitawar neuromuscular (matsayi, aikin tsari) yana nuna haɓakawa a cikin makonni 4-8. Daidaitawar tsari (taurin tsoka daga plyometrics) yana ɗaukar makonni 8-12. Daidaitawar motsa jiki (yawan mitochondrial daga babban nisa) yana buƙatar watanni 3-6 na daidaiton horo.
