Menene Gudun rTSS (rTSS)?

Makin Damuwa Mai Gudu (rTSS)yana ƙididdige nauyin horo na motsa jiki mai gudana ta hanyar haɗa ƙarfi da tsawon lokaci. An daidaita shi daga tsarin TSS na keke, ta amfani da Mahimmancin ku Gudun Gudun (CRS) azaman madaidaicin matakin. Aikin motsa jiki na awa 1 a taki CRS = 100 rTSS.

Yadda ake ƙididdige rTSS

Formula

rTSS = (Tsawon lokaci a cikin sa'o'i) × (Maganin Ƙarfi)² × 100

Inda:

  • Halin Ƙarfi (IF)= CRS Taki / Matsakaicin Taki
  • Tsawon lokaci= Jimlar lokacin motsa jiki a cikin sa'o'i
  • CRS Taki= Matsakaicin matakin ku daga gwajin CRS

Jagororin rTSS na mako-mako

Manufar mako-mako rTSS ya dogara da matakin horo da burin ku:

Masu Gudun Nishaɗi

rTSS na mako-mako:150-300

2-3 motsa jiki a kowane mako, 50-100 rTSS kowanne. Mayar da hankali kan fasaha da gina ginin aerobic.

Masu Gudun Lafiyar Jiki / Triathletes

rTSS na mako-mako:300-500

3-4 motsa jiki a kowane mako, 75-125 rTSS kowanne. Haɗuwa da jimiri na aerobic da aikin ƙofa.

Gasar Masters Runners

rTSS na mako-mako:500-800

4-6 motsa jiki a kowane mako, 80-150 rTSS kowanne. Horon da aka tsara tare da lokaci-lokaci.

Elite / Collegiate Runners

rTSS na mako-mako:800-1200+

8-12 motsa jiki a kowane mako, kwana biyu. Babban girma tare da sarrafa farfadowa mai mahimmanci.

⚠️ Muhimman Bayani

  • Yana buƙatar ingantaccen CRS:Dole ne CRS ɗin ku ya kasance na yanzu (an gwada shi a cikin makonni 6-8) don daidai rTSS.
  • Ƙididdigar Sauƙaƙe:Wannan kalkuleta yana amfani da matsakaita taki. Advanced rTSS yana amfani Matsakaicin Matsayi na Graded (NGP) wanda ke lissafin tsarin tazara.
  • Ba don aikin fasaha ba:rTSS kawai yana auna damuwa na horo na jiki, ba fasaha ba ci gaba.
  • Bambance-bambancen mutum:rTSS iri ɗaya yana jin daban don masu gudu daban-daban. Daidaita jagororin dangane da farfadowar ku.

Me yasa rTSS Mahimmanci

Sakamakon Horon Danniya shine tushen don:

  • CTL (Nauyin Horon na yau da kullun):Matsayin lafiyar ku - matsakaicin nauyi na kwanaki 42 na yau da kullun rTSS
  • ATL (Ƙarar Ƙarfin Horarwa):Gajiyarku - matsakaicin nauyi na kwanaki 7 na yau da kullun KIYAYE14X
  • TSB (Ma'aunin Damuwa na Horarwa):Fom ɗin ku - TSB = CTL - ATL (mai kyau = sabo, korau = gajiya)
  • Tsawon lokaci:Shirya matakan horo (tushe, gini, kololuwa, taper) ta amfani da manufa CTL ci gaba
  • Gudanar da farfadowa:Sanin lokacin turawa da lokacin hutawa bisa TSB

Pro Tukwici: Bibiya CTL

Yi rikodin kullun rTSS a cikin maƙunsar rubutu ko log ɗin horo. Yi lissafin matsakaicin kwanaki 42 (CTL) mako-mako. manufa Maki 5-10 CTL yana ƙaruwa a kowane mako yayin ginin tushe. Ci gaba ko rage ƴan CTL yayin tafe (1-2 makonni kafin tseren).

Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi

Menene Gudun TSS (rTSS)?

Gudun Horon Stress Score (rTSS) ma'auni ne wanda ke ƙididdige nauyin horon Gudun motsa jiki ta hanyar haɗa duka ƙarfi da tsawon lokaci. An daidaita shi daga TSS na keke Hanyar, ta yin amfani da Matsakaicin Gudun Gudunku (CRS) azaman matakin kofa. Motsa jiki na awa 1 a gudun CRS yayi daidai da 100 rTSS.

Ta yaya zan lissafta rTSS na?

Yi amfani da kalkuleta da ke sama ta shigar da taki na CRS (daga gwajin CRS), jimlar lokacin motsa jiki, da matsakaicin taki yayin motsa jiki. Ma'anar ita ce: rTSS = Tsawon lokaci (awa) × Ƙarfi Factor³ × 100, inda Factor Factor = CRS Taki / Matsakaicin Taki.

Ina bukatan CRS don lissafta rTSS?

Ee, Ana buƙatar Gudun Gudun Mahimmancin ku (CRS) don ƙididdige Factor Factor, wanda shine mahimmanci don lissafin rTSS. CRS yana wakiltar saurin kofa kuma yakamata a gwada shi kowane 6-8 makonni. Kuna iya nemo CRS ta amfani da muCRS kalkuleta.

Menene kyakkyawan maki rTSS don motsa jiki ɗaya?

Ya dogara da ƙarfin motsa jiki: Ayyukan motsa jiki masu sauƙi yawanci maki ƙasa da 50 rTSS, matsakaici motsa jiki 50-100 rTSS, motsa jiki mai tsanani 100-200 rTSS, da kuma motsa jiki mai wuyar gaske sama da 200 rTSS. The maki mai dacewa ya dogara da burin horonku da matakin dacewa na yanzu.

Nawa rTSS zan yi kowane mako?

Makasudin mako-mako rTSS sun bambanta da matakin: Masu gudu na nishaɗi: 150-300, Fitness masu tsere / 'yan wasan uku: 300-500, Masters masu gasa: 500-800, Elite/Collegiate: 800-1200+. Fara da ra'ayin mazan jiya kuma ƙara a hankali don guje wa wuce gona da iri.

Shin yana gudana TSS daidai yake da TSS na keke?

Manufar da dabara iri ɗaya ne, amma rTSS an daidaita shi don gudana. Maimakon amfani da iko (FTP) kamar hawan keke TSS, rTSS yana amfani da taki tare da CRS azaman bakin kofa. Dukansu suna auna nauyin horo Tsawon lokaci × Ƙarfin Ƙarfafawa² × 100.

Shin rTSS yana lissafin ƙasa ko tuddai?

Madaidaicin rTSS dangane da matsakaita tafiyar GPS baya lissafin tuddai. Domin gudu ko hanyoyin tuddai, kayan aikin ci-gaba suna amfani da suMatsakaicin Matsakaicin Matsayi (NGP)ko bugun zuciya tazara don samar da ingantaccen wakilci na damuwa na horo.

Menene bambanci tsakanin rTSS da CTL/ATL/TSB?

rTSS yana auna nauyin horo guda ɗaya.CTL (Load ɗin Horon Horon na yau da kullun) shine dogon lokacin ku dacewa, ATL (Babban Horon Load) shine gajiyarku na kwanan nan, kuma TSB (Ma'auni na Matsala na Horarwa) shine sabo. Waɗannan ma'auni suna amfani da ƙimar rTSS akan lokaci don bin yanayin horon ku. Ƙara koyo a cikin namuJagoran lodin horo.
