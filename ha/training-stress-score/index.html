Menene Makin Damuwa na Koyarwa (TSS)?

Makin Damuwa na horo lamba ɗaya ce wacce ke wakiltar nauyin horo gabaɗaya na motsa jiki, lissafin kuɗi duka tsawon lokaci da tsanani. Ba kamar kawai bin diddigin mil ko lokaci ba, TSS ma'aunin nauyi daidai yake: a Gudun ɗan lokaci na minti 60 yana haifar da ƙarin damuwa na horo fiye da gudu mai sauƙi na mintuna 60.

Mabuɗin Ka'idodin Bayan TSS

  • Ƙididdigar Maƙasudin:TSS yana ba da daidaitaccen awo don kwatanta daban motsa jiki - tazara vs dan lokaci vs dogon gudu
  • Girman Nauyin:Ayyukan motsa jiki mafi girma suna haifar da damuwa da yawa fiye da tsawon lokaci kadai zai ba da shawarar
  • Tarin Bin-sawu:Ana iya taƙaita TSS kowace rana, mako-mako, da kuma fiye da hawan horo don bin jimlar nauyin horo
  • Daidaiton Mutum:TSS an keɓance shi zuwa bakin kofa, yana sanya shi keɓance ga naka matakin dacewa
  • Gudanar da Gajiya:Ma'auni da aka samo TSS (CTL, ATL, TSB) yana hasashen dacewa, gajiya, da mafi kyau nauyin horo

rTSS: TSS don Gudu

Yayin da aka fara haɓaka TSS ta amfani da bayanan wutar lantarki, rTSS (Makin Ƙarfafa Ƙwararru) ra'ayi don gudana ta amfani da taki maimakon iko. Tsarin tsari na asali ya kasance iri ɗaya, amma rTSS yana amfani da shi kuGudun Mahimmanci (CRS)ko kofa taki a matsayin tushe.

Me yasa CRS don rTSS?

CRS yana wakiltar saurin canjin yanayi mai ɗorewa na aerobic-anaerobic-mahimmancin "tafin ƙofar aiki." Yin amfani da CRS azaman ma'anar tunani yana tabbatar da:

  • Ƙididdigar ƙididdige ƙididdiga ta musamman ga ilimin halittar ku
  • Daidaitaccen kwatancen matakan iya gudu daban-daban
  • Daidaitaccen ma'auni na ƙarfi dangane da ainihin iyawar ku
  • Daidaito tare da ingantattun ma'auni na kimiyya

Madadin alamar kofa sun haɗa da:

  • Takin gwaji na mintuna 30:Tafin ƙofa mai aiki daga gwajin lokaci na mintuna 30
  • Gudun tsere 10K + 10-15 sec/mile:Ƙidaya mai ƙima daga 10K na baya-bayan nan
  • Matsakaicin lactate:Daga dakin gwaje-gwaje ko gwajin lactate na filin

Koyi yadda ake gwada CRS a cikin namujagorar gwajin aiki.

Yadda ake lissafin rTSS

Tsarin rTSS yana lissafin duka tsawon lokacin motsa jiki da ƙarfi dangane da bakin kofa:

rTSS = (Duration in seconds × IF² × 100) / 3600

Where:
IF = Intensity Factor (workout pace / CRS pace)
Duration = Total workout time in seconds

Misalin Ƙididdigar mataki-mataki

Aikin motsa jiki:Tsawon minti 60 yana gudu a 4:00/km taki
CRS naku:4:20/km (270m/min ko 4.5m/s)

Mataki 1: Maida taki zuwa m/s

  • Takin motsa jiki: 4:00/km = 4.17 m/s
  • CRS taki: 4:20/km = 3.85 m/s

Mataki na 2: Lissafin Ƙarfin Ƙarfafa (IF)

IF = Workout pace / CRS pace
IF = 4.17 / 3.85 = 1.08

Mataki na 3: Yi lissafin rTSS

Duration = 60 minutes = 3,600 seconds
rTSS = (3,600 × 1.08² × 100) / 3,600
rTSS = (3,600 × 1.166 × 100) / 3,600
rTSS = 116.6 ≈ 117

Wannan ɗan gajeren lokaci na mintuna 60 yana gudana a 108% na CRS yana haifar da 117 rTSS - ɗan ƙaramin damuwa fiye da sa'a ɗaya a bakin kofa. samar (wanda yayi daidai da 100 TSS ta ma'anar).

rTSS don Haɗaɗɗen Aiki

Don motsa jiki tare da ɓangarori da yawa a mabambantan ƙarfi daban-daban (tazara, gudanawar ci gaba), ƙididdige TSS don kowane bangare kuma tara su:

Misali: Tazarar motsa jiki

  • 15 min dumama (60% CRS): IF = 0.60, TSS = 9
  • 6 × 4 min a 110% CRS (minti 24 duka): IF = 1.10, TSS = 48
  • Farfadowa tsakanin tazara (minti 12 a 50% CRS): IF = 0.50, TSS = 2
  • 10 min sanyi (60% CRS): IF = 0.60, TSS = 6

Fahimtar Ƙarfin Ƙarfafawa (IF)

Ƙarfafa Factor shine rabon takun motsa jiki zuwa matakin kofa. Yana wakiltar irin wahalar da kuke aiki dangane da iyakar ƙoƙarin ku na aerobic mai dorewa.

Dangantakar ma'auni (IF²) tana nuna haɓakar haɓakar damuwa na ilimin lissafi yayin da ƙarfi ya tashi:

CTL, ATL, da TSB Metrics

TSS don motsa jiki ɗaya yana da amfani, amma ainihin ƙarfin yana fitowa ne daga bin diddigin ma'auni na tsawon lokaci. Uku Abubuwan da aka samu suna sarrafa nauyin horo na dogon lokaci:

Load ɗin Horarwa na yau da kullun (CTL): Jiyya

CTL shine nauyin horonku na dogon lokaci, wanda aka ƙididdige shi azaman matsakaicin nauyi na kwanaki 42 na yau da kullun TSS. CTL yana wakiltar dacewar ku - ƙarfin ku don ɗaukar damuwa na horo.

CTL(today) = CTL(yesterday) + (TSS(today) - CTL(yesterday)) / 42
  • Mafi girma CTL:Mafi dacewa da ƙarfin aiki
  • Ƙara CTL:Gina lafiyar jiki (amma kuma yana tara gajiya)
  • Stable CTL:Kula da matakin dacewa
  • Ragewar CTL:Detraining (da gangan taper ko mara shiri)

Maɗaukakin Horarwa Mai Sauƙi (ATL): Gajiya

ATL shine nauyin horonku na ɗan gajeren lokaci, wanda aka ƙididdige shi azaman matsakaicin nauyi na kwanaki 7 na yau da kullun TSS. ATL yana wakiltar gajiyar ku - tasirin horon kwanan nan.

ATL(today) = ATL(yesterday) + (TSS(today) - ATL(yesterday)) / 7
  • Mafi girma ATL:Kara tara gajiya
  • ATL> CTL:Gajiya mai mahimmanci dangane da dacewa (haɗarin wuce gona da iri)
  • ATL << CTL:An murmure da kyau, shirye don horo mai wahala

Ma'aunin Damuwa na Horarwa (TSB): Form

TSB shine bambanci tsakanin dacewa da gajiya. Yana tsinkayar nau'in ku - shirye-shiryen yin aiki ko horarwa sosai.

TSB = CTL - ATL
  • TSB = -30 zuwa -10:Babban gajiya, lokaci mai yawa da yawa
  • TSB = -10 zuwa +5:Mafi kyawun kewayon horo, ma'auni mai kyau
  • TSB = +5 zuwa +15:Sabo, tafe, shirye-shiryen tsere
  • TSB = +15 zuwa +25:Sabo sosai, tsari kololuwa don tseren maɓalli
  • TSB> +25:Detraining, rasa dacewa

TSS Jagoran Rago

Ci gaba da ɗorawa yana buƙatar haɓakar TSS madaidaiciya, amma saurin haɓaka yana haifar da rauni da wuce gona da iri:

  • Matsakaicin karuwa na mako-mako:5-10% karuwa a mako-mako TSS
  • Matsakaicin haɓaka CTL:maki 5-8 CTL kowane mako
  • Makonni farfadowa:Kowane mako 3-4, rage TSS da 30-50%
  • Raunin / rashin lafiya:Bayan sallama, komawa a 50% TSS da ta gabata, ƙara 10% a kowane mako

Makon farfadowa TSS

Makonni na farfadowa da aka tsara sun hana horarwa da ba da damar daidaitawa:

  • Mitar:Kowane mako 3-4
  • Rage TSS:30-50% na makon da ya gabata
  • Kulawa mai ƙarfi:Rike wasu ingantaccen aiki amma rage girma sosai
  • Tasirin CTL:Ƙananan CTL raguwa (maki 1-3) amma gagarumin raguwar ATL (maki 10-20)
  • TSB ingantawa:TSB yakamata ya tashi zuwa +5 zuwa +15 yayin makon dawowa

Amfani da TSS don Gudanar da Horon

TSS yana canzawa daga bayanai masu ban sha'awa zuwa jagora mai aiki lokacin amfani da shawarar horo.

Tsare-tsaren mako-mako TSS

  1. Kafa tushen tushe:Bibiyar makonni 2-3 na horo na yanzu don ƙayyade makowa mai dorewa TSS
  2. Saita manufa CTL:Ƙayyade burin CTL don tseren manufa (duba tebur a sama)
  3. Yi lissafin TSS da ake buƙata:Yi aiki baya daga burin CTL don tantance mako-mako TSS da ake buƙata
  4. Ƙimar haɓakar shirin:Ƙara TSS kowane mako da 5-10% matsakaicin kowane mako
  5. Jadawalin dawowa:Kowane mako 3-4, rage TSS da 30-50%

Misali: Gina daga CTL 50 zuwa CTL 85 don marathon

  • CTL na yanzu: 50 (na yau da kullun TSS ~ 350)
  • Manufar CTL: 85 (yana buƙatar TSS ~595 kowane mako)
  • Ana samun makonni: makonni 16
  • Ƙarar CTL da ake buƙata: maki 35
  • Matsakaicin ɗorewa: ~ 2.2 CTL maki a kowane mako
  • Ci gaban TSS na mako-mako: 350 → 385 → 420 → 455 → 490 → 525 → 560 → 595
  • Tare da makwanni na dawowa: Cimma maƙasudin CTL tare da buffer don taper

Kuskuren TSS gama gari

1. Neman Lambobin TSS

Matsala:Kula da TSS azaman maki don haɓakawa maimakon kayan aikin gudanarwa.

Me yasa ba daidai ba:Ƙarin TSS ba koyaushe ya fi kyau ba. Matsanancin TSS ba tare da isassun murmurewa ba yana haifar da overtraining, rauni, da raguwar aiki.

Magani:Yi amfani da TSS don tabbatar da isasshen kuzari yayin mutunta buƙatun dawowa. Abubuwan inganci fiye da yawa.

2. Yin watsi da Rarraba Ƙarfi

Matsala:Buga maƙasudin mako-mako TSS tare da yawancin horon "yankin launin toka" Zone 3.

Me yasa ba daidai ba:500 mako-mako TSS daga galibin aikin Zone 3 yana haifar da sakamako mafi muni fiye da 500 TSS tare da daidai 80/20 rarraba. Kuna tara gajiya ba tare da isassun ƙarfin horo ko sauƙi ba.

Magani:Bi TSS ta yanki. Tabbatar cewa 75-85% ya zo daga saurin gudu, tare da aiki tuƙuru da gaske (Yanki na 4-5).

3. Rapid CTL Ramping

Matsala:Haɓaka CTL da maki 10+ a kowane mako don "kama" kan dacewa.

Me yasa ba daidai ba:Haɗuwa da nama mai haɗawa ya zama karbuwa na zuciya da jijiyoyin jini. Mai sauri TSS yana ƙaruwa haifar da rauni ko da kun ji aerobically shirye don kaya.

Magani:Iyakance CTL yana ƙaruwa zuwa maki 5-8 a kowane mako. Haɗa makonnin dawowa kowane 3-4 makonni.

4. Yin watsi da Martanin Mutum

Matsala:Bin jagororin TSS na gabaɗaya ba tare da daidaitawa don ƙarfin dawo da mutum ɗaya ba.

Me yasa ba daidai ba:Farfadowa ya bambanta da shekaru, tarihin horo, ingancin barci, damuwa, da kwayoyin halitta. A Makon 500 TSS na iya zama cikakke ga mai gudu ɗaya kuma mai ƙarfi ga wani matakin ƙarfin iri ɗaya.

Magani:Yi amfani da jagororin TSS azaman wuraren farawa. Kula da aiki, barci, kuzari, da bugun zuciya mai hutawa. Daidaita makasudin TSS dangane da martanin mutum ɗaya.

5. Ƙimar Ƙimar TSS na waje

Matsala:Dogaro da ƙididdigar smartwatch TSS ba tare da fahimtar hanyoyin lissafi ba.

Me yasa ba daidai ba:Yawancin agogo suna amfani da algorithms na mallaka waɗanda ba su daidaita da daidaitaccen TSS ba hanya. Wasu sun wuce gona da iri, wasu kuma suna raina. Ƙididdigar da ba ta dace ba ta sa bin diddigin abin ya zama abin dogaro.

Magani:Yi amfani da madaidaicin hanyar ƙididdigewa bisa CRS ko takin kofa. MurTSS kalkuletayana amfani da ingantattun hanyoyin don daidaito, daidaito sakamako.

6. Kwatanta TSS Tsakanin Masu Gudu

Matsala:Ɗaukar daidai TSS yana nufin daidaitaccen ƙarfin horo ga masu gudu daban-daban.

Me yasa ba daidai ba:TSS yana da alaƙa da kowane kofa. Aikin motsa jiki na 100 TSS don ƙwararren mai gudu (misali, mintuna 60 a 5:30/mil ƙofa taki) ya bambanta sosai daga 100 TSS don mai gudu na nishaɗi (60) Minti a 9:00/mil ƙofa).

Magani:Yi amfani da TSS kawai don kwatanta kai akan lokaci. Kar a taɓa kwatanta cikakkiyar ƙimar TSS tsakanin 'yan wasa.

7. Yin watsi da damuwa mara Gudu

Matsala:Sarrafa TSS daidai amma yin watsi da damuwa na rayuwa, buƙatun aiki, da abubuwan da ba sa gudu.

Me yasa ba daidai ba:Jimlar damuwa (ba kawai damuwa na horo ba) yana ƙayyade bukatun dawowa da yi. Babban damuwa na rayuwa + babban TSS = overtraining.

Magani:Rage makasudin TSS yayin lokacin rayuwa mai tsananin damuwa. Saka idanu HRV, ingancin barci, da gajiya na zahiri. Daidaita horo bisa jimlar nauyin damuwa, ba kawai TSS ba.

Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi

Menene kyakkyawan TSS na dogon lokaci?

Dogon gudu TSS ya bambanta ta tsawon lokaci da taki. Horon marathon na al'ada na dogon lokaci (mil 16-20 a cikin sauƙi mai sauƙi, IF 0.75-0.80) yana haifar da 150-250 TSS. Gudu mai tsayi sosai (mil 20+) ko sauri mai tsayi zai iya kaiwa 250-350 TSS. Dogon gudu yakamata ya wakilci 25-35% na mako-mako TSS a yawancin tsare-tsaren horo.

Nawa TSS yayi yawa a cikin motsa jiki ɗaya?

Ayyukan motsa jiki guda ɗaya TSS sama da 300 yana buƙatar dawowar kwanaki 2-3; sama da 450 yana buƙatar kwanaki 4-7. Yawancin motsa jiki na horo ya zama 80-200 TSS. Ajiye ayyukan motsa jiki 300+ TSS don dogon gudu, tsere, ko takamaiman horon lokaci. Ƙari Fiye da motsa jiki na 300+ TSS a kowane mako yana haifar da gajiya mai yawa ga yawancin masu gudu.

Zan iya ƙididdige TSS don gudu-gudu?

Ee, lissafin TSS yana aiki iri ɗaya don injin tuƙi yana gudana ta amfani da bayanan taki. Duk da haka, ya kamata a yi takin tela saita zuwa karkata 1% don dacewa da ƙoƙarin waje. Idan kwatanta injin tuƙi da na waje TSS, tabbatar da daidaito — kar a yi canza tsakanin hanyoyin kuma tsammanin ƙima iri ɗaya, kamar yadda injiniyoyi masu gudana suka bambanta kaɗan.

Shin zan iya ƙidaya horon giciye a cikin mako-mako TSS?

Don ƙayyadaddun bayanai, ƙididdige gudu TSS daban da horon giciye. Keke keke da sauran ayyuka haifar da danniya horo amma daban-daban sabawa. Idan kuna bin jimlar nauyin horo a duk wasanni, yi amfani ware TSS lissafin ga kowane. Yawancin masu gudu suna amfana daga bin diddigin TSS na musamman.

Ta yaya zan saita CRS na don ingantaccen lissafin rTSS?

Yi gwajin CRS da ya dace: 3-minti 3 da matsakaicin gwaji na 7 tare da dawo da mintuna 30 tsakanin. Yi ƙididdige CRS = (D7 - D3) / 4. A sake gwadawa kowane mako 6-8 yayin da dacewa ta inganta. A madadin, yi amfani da lokacin minti 30 saurin gwaji ko gudun tseren 10K + 10-15 seconds kowace mil. Madaidaicin CRS yana da mahimmanci don ma'anar TSS bayanai. Duba muCRS kalkuletadon cikakkun ka'idojin gwaji.

Menene TSB zan yi niyya don tseren manufa?

Manufar TSB na +15 zuwa +25 don tseren fifiko na A. Don tseren B (tun-ups), TSB na +5 zuwa +15 ya dace. C tseren (Gudun horo) ana iya yin shi a TSB na -5 zuwa +5. Mafi girma TSB (sabo) yana inganta aiki amma yana buƙatar tsayi tafe. Daidaita mahimmancin tsere akan ci gaba da horo lokacin saita maƙasudin TSB.

Yaya sauri CTL ke lalacewa yayin rauni?

CTL yana raguwa a kusan maki 1 a kowace rana na cikakken hutu, ko maki 7 a kowane mako. Duk da haka, physiological motsa jiki yana raguwa a hankali fiye da yadda CTL ya nuna. Bayan hutun makonni 2 (digowar CTL na ~ 14 maki), ainihin rashin lafiyar jiki kawai 5-10%. Horon-giciye na iya kula da 50-70% na motsa jiki mai gudana, rage lalata CTL. Lokacin dawowa daga rauni, sake gina CTL a hankali a maki 3-5 a kowane mako.

Shin TSS daidai ne don guduwar hanya da tsaunuka?

TSS na tushen taki ba shi da inganci akan hanyoyi da manyan tsaunuka saboda saurin ba ya nuna ƙoƙari. Don hanya Gudun, tushen bugun zuciya hrTSS yana ba da daidaito mafi kyau. A madadin, yi amfani da tushen wutar lantarki TSS idan kuna da Mitar wutar lantarki mai gudana — lissafin wutar lantarki don matsayi da ƙasa. Don titin tudu da ke gudana tare da GPS, tushen taki TSS ya kasance daidai daidai akan cikakken tsawon lokacin motsa jiki.

Zan iya amfani da TSS tare da yankunan bugun zuciya maimakon taki?

Ee, hrTSS yana amfani da bugun zuciya dangane da LTHR (ƙarfin bugun zuciya na lactate) maimakon taki dangane da CRS. Ana ƙididdige tsari kamar: hrTSS = (Lokaci × IF²) / 36, inda IF = matsakaicin HR / LTHR. hrTSS yana aiki da kyau don ƙasa inda taki ba abin dogaro ba ne. Koyaya, motsin zuciya na iya haɓaka hrTSS akan dogon gudu-tushen rTSS yana da ƙari. m ga lebur, auna darussa.

Menene dangantakar dake tsakanin TSS da nisan mil na mako-mako?

TSS da mileage suna daidaita amma ba sa canzawa. Makon mil 50 a cikin sauƙi mai sauƙi (IF 0.75) yana haifar da ~ 450 TSS. Irin wannan mil 50 tare da matsanancin motsa jiki na iya haifar da 550-650 TSS. TSS yana lissafin girman wannan nisan shi kadai ke kewar. Masu gudu biyu masu nisan mako guda iri ɗaya na iya samun nau'ikan TSS daban-daban da matakan gajiya. dangane da tsananin rarraba. Yi amfani da ma'auni biyu tare don cikakken hoto.
