Run Analytics vs Strava

Abin da Strava yayi kyau

  • Siffofin zamantakewa:Clubs, sassa, godiya, ciyarwar ayyuka
  • Bibiyar wasanni da yawa:Gudu, keke, gudu, yawo, da sauransu.
  • Matakin kyauta:Fasalolin kyauta masu karimci ga 'yan wasa na yau da kullun
  • Babban tushen mai amfani:Haɗa tare da miliyoyin 'yan wasa a duniya
  • Haɗin Apple Watch:Daidaita kai tsaye daga motsa jiki

Menene Run Analytics Yayi Kyau

  • Ƙayyadaddun ma'auni masu gudana:CRS, rTSS, yankunan taki da aka tsara don waƙoƙi
  • Binciken lodin horo:CTL/ATL/TSB hada (Strava bashi da wannan)
  • Atomatik rTSS:Babu shigarwar bayanan hannu, ƙididdiga daga CRS + taki
  • Makanikan tafiya:DPS, ƙimar tafiya, bin diddigin matakai
  • Yankunan horo:Yankunan tafiya 6 na keɓaɓɓen dangane da ilimin halittar ku

Hukunci: Run Analytics vs Strava

Yi amfani da Strava idan:Kuna son fasalulluka na zamantakewa, bin diddigin wasanni da yawa, ko bin sawu na yau da kullun kyauta. Strava yana da kyau don shiga motsa jiki da haɗawa da abokai.

Yi amfani da Run Analytics idan:Kuna da gaske game da gudanar da aiki kuma kuna son yankunan tushen CRS, atomatik rTSS, da kuma sarrafa nauyin horo (CTL/ATL/TSB). Strava baya lissafin gudu TSS ko samar da ma'aunin PMC.

Yi amfani da duka biyu:Yawancin masu gudu suna amfani da Strava don rabawa jama'a da Run Analytics don bin diddigin ayyuka. Suna karawa juna kyau.

Yi amfani da Run Analytics idan:Kai mai gudu ne (ba mai wasan triathlete ba) ko kuna son takamaiman ma'aunin gudu ba tare da biyan $20 / mo ba. Run Analytics yana ba da CTL/ATL/TSB da rTSS lissafin 80% ƙasa da farashi fiye da HoroPeaks Premium.

Bambanci mai mahimmanci:TrainingPeaks wasa ne da yawa tare da fasalin horarwa; Gudu Ana gudanar da bincike-kawai tare da goyan bayan CRS na asali da samun damar PMC mai araha.

Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi

Zan iya amfani da Run Analytics DA Strava/TrainingPeaks?

Ee-masu gudu da yawa suna amfani da duka biyun.Yi amfani da Run Analytics don nazarin ayyuka (CRS, rTSS, PMC) da Strava don rabawa jama'a da shiga wasanni da yawa. Suna karawa juna kyau.

Shin Run Analytics yana aiki tare da agogon Garmin?

A'a.Run Analytics yana daidaitawa ta hanyar Apple Health, wanda ke buƙatar Apple Watch. Idan kuna amfani da Garmin, yi la'akari da Kololuwar Training ko Ƙarshe na Ƙarshe maimakon.

Me yasa Run Analytics ya fi arha fiye da Kololuwar Training?

Run Analytics negudu-kawai, ba wasanni da yawa ba. Ta hanyar mayar da hankali kan gudu, mu guje wa rikitarwa da tsadar ababen more rayuwa na tallafawa mitocin wutar lantarki, motsa jiki, koci dandamali, da sauransu. Wannan yana ba mu damar bayar da PMC + rTSS a 80% ƙananan farashi.

Idan ni dan wasan triathlete ne fa - shin zan yi amfani da Run Analytics?

Wataƙila ba a matsayin farkon app ɗin ku ba.Triathletes suna amfana daga dandamali na wasanni da yawa kamar Kololuwar horarwa waɗanda ke bin keke, gudu, da gudu tare. Koyaya, wasu 'yan wasan triathletes suna amfani da Run Analytics don ƙayyadaddun ƙididdiga masu gudana (yankunan CRS) da Kololuwar horo don ɗaukar nauyin horo gabaɗaya.

Shin Run Analytics yana da matakin kyauta?

Run Analytics yana ba da aGwajin kyauta na kwanaki 7tare da cikakkun siffofi (gwajin CRS, rTSS, PMC). Bayan gwaji, yana da $3.99/mo ba tare da dogon lokaci ba. Babu matakin kyauta - mun yi imanin 'yan wasa sun cancanci cika nazari ba tare da makullin fasalin sabani ba.
