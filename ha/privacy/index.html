Manufar Keɓantawa don Run Analytics

An sabunta ta ƙarshe:Janairu 10, 2025 |Mai tasiri Kwanan wata:Janairu 10, 2025

Gabatarwa

Run Analytics ("mu," "namu," ko "app") an himmatu don kare sirrin ku. Wannan Manufar Sirri yayi bayanin yadda aikace-aikacen mu ta hannu (iOS da Android) shiga, amfani, da kare bayanan lafiya daga naku na'urar.

Mabuɗin Ƙa'idar Sirri:Run Analytics yana aiki akan asifili-uwar garke, gida-kawai gine-gine. Duk bayanan lafiya da aka samu daga Apple HealthKit (iOS) ko Haɗin Lafiya (Android) ya kasance na musamman akan na'urar ku ta zahiri kuma ba a taɓa watsa shi zuwa sabar waje, sabis na girgije, ko wasu kamfanoni.

1. Samun Bayanan Lafiya

Run Analytics yana haɗawa tare da dandamalin lafiyar na'urar ku don samar da cikakken aikin motsa jiki bincike:

1.1 iOS - Haɗin Apple HealthKit

A kan na'urorin iOS, Run Analytics yana haɗawa daApple HealthKitdon samun damar motsa jiki mai gudana bayanai. Muna buƙatar samun damar karantawa kawai zuwa:

  • Zaurukan motsa jiki:Gudun zaman motsa jiki tare da lokaci da tsawon lokaci
  • Nisa:Jimlar nisan gudu
  • Yawan Zuciya:Bayanan bugun zuciya yayin motsa jiki
  • Makamashi Mai Aiki:Calories sun ƙone a lokacin zaman gudu
  • Ƙididdiga Mataki:Bayanan mataki don lissafin adadin

Amincewar Apple HealthKit:Run Analytics yana bin duk ƙa'idodin Apple HealthKit. Ana sarrafa bayanan lafiyar ku gaba ɗaya akan na'urar iOS kuma baya barin ta. Ba mu taɓa raba HealthKit ba bayanai tare da wasu kamfanoni, dandamalin talla, ko dillalan bayanai.

1.2 Android - Haɗin Haɗin Lafiya

Nau'in Bayanan LafiyaIziniManufar
Zaman Motsa jikiREAD_EXERCISEDon ganowa da shigo da zaman motsa jiki masu gudana daga Haɗin Lafiya
Rubutun NisaREAD_DISTANCEDon nuna ma'auni na maɓalli kamar jimlar tazarar gudu, rarrabuwa, da lissafin taki
Rubutun Ƙimar ZuciyaREAD_HEART_RATEDon nuna sigogin bugun zuciya, ƙididdige matsakaici da matsakaicin ƙimar zuciya yayin motsa jiki

Izinin Android:Ana buƙatar waɗannan izini yayin ƙaddamar da ƙa'idar ta farko. Kai na iya soke waɗannan izini a kowane lokaci ta hanyar Saitunan Android → Apps → Haɗin Lafiya → Run Bincike.

1.3 Yadda Muke Amfani da Bayanan Lafiya

Ana amfani da duk bayanan lafiyana musamman don dalilai masu zuwa:

  • Nunin Ayyukan motsa jiki:Nuna zaman tafiyarku tare da cikakkun ma'auni (nisa, lokaci, taki, bugun zuciya)
  • Binciken Ayyuka:Ƙirƙirar yankunan taki, ƙididdigar ƙididdiga, Takin Ƙarfi, da rTSS (Gudun Makin Damuwar Koyarwa)
  • Bibiyar Ci gaba:Nuna yanayin aiki, mafi kyawun mutum, da motsa jiki taƙaitaccen bayani
  • Export Data:Bada izinin fitar da bayanan motsa jiki zuwa tsarin CSV don amfanin kai

1.4 Adana Bayanai

🔒 GARANTIN KIRKI MAI MUHIMMAN:

Duk bayanan lafiya ya rage keɓancewar akan na'urar ku ta zahiri.

  • iOS:Ana adana bayanai ta amfani da iOS Core Data da UserDefaults (kan na'ura kawai)
  • Android:Ana adana bayanai ta amfani da Android Room Database (kan na'urar SQLite)
  • BABU bayanai da aka loda zuwa sabar waje
  • BABU bayanai da ake watsa ta hanyar intanet
  • BABU aiki tare da girgije ko ajiyar bayanan lafiya
  • BABU damar shiga bayanan lafiyar ku

Iyakar lokacin data bar na'urarka shine yausheKA zaɓa a sararidon fitarwa naku motsa jiki zuwa tsarin CSV kuma raba fayil ɗin da kanka.

2. Ana Bukatar Izini

2.1 iOS Izinin

  • Samun damar HealthKit:Karanta samun damar motsa jiki, Nisa, Ƙimar Zuciya, Ƙarfi Mai Aiki, da Matakai
  • Laburaren Hoto (Na zaɓi):Sai kawai idan kun zaɓi adana taƙaitaccen aikin motsa jiki azaman hotuna

Kuna iya sarrafa izinin HealthKit a kowane lokaci a cikin Saitunan iOS → Keɓantawa & Tsaro → Lafiya → Gudu Bincike.

2.2 Izinin Android

  • android.permission.health.READ_EXERCISE
  • android.permission.health.READ_DISTANCE
  • android.permission.health.READ_HEART_RATE
  • android.permission.health.READ_SPEED
  • android.permission.health.READ_STEPS
  • android.permission.health.READ_TOTAL_CALORIES_BURNED
  • Samun Intanet(INTERNET): Ana amfani da shi kawai don nuna tsayayyen in-app abun ciki da samun damar sarrafa biyan kuɗi (Biyan Kuɗi na Google Play). Ba a watsa bayanan lafiya.
  • Sabis na gaba(FOREGROUND_SERVICE): Don yiwuwar nan gaba fasalulluka daidaitawa na baya (ba a aiwatar da su a halin yanzu).

4. In-App Siyayya da Biyan Kuɗi

Run Analytics yana ba da zaɓi na biyan kuɗi na in-app wanda aka sarrafa ta tsarin biyan kuɗi na asali na na'urarku:

4.1 iOS - App Store Biyan kuɗi

Lokacin da kuka sayi biyan kuɗi akan iOS:

  • Apple yana sarrafa duk sarrafa biyan kuɗi ta hanyar App Store
  • Muna karɓar matsayin biyan kuɗi kawai (aiki/mara aiki) ta StoreKit
  • Ba mu da damar yin amfani da bayanan biyan kuɗin ku (katin kuɗi, adireshin lissafin kuɗi)
  • Ana adana bayanan biyan kuɗi a gida akan na'urar ku

Sarrafa biyan kuɗi:

  • Saitunan iOS → Sunanka → Biyan kuɗi → Run Analytics
  • Ko a cikin app: Saituna → Sarrafa Biyan kuɗi

4.2 Android - Google Play Billing

Lokacin da ka sayi biyan kuɗi akan Android:

  • Google Play yana sarrafa duk sarrafa biyan kuɗi
  • Muna karɓar matsayin biyan kuɗi kawai (aiki/marasa aiki) ta Google Play Billing API
  • Ba mu da damar yin amfani da bayanan biyan kuɗin ku (katin kuɗi, adireshin lissafin kuɗi)
  • Ana adana bayanan biyan kuɗi a gida akan na'urar ku

Sarrafa biyan kuɗi:

  • Google Play Store → Account → Biyan kuɗi → Run Analytics
  • Ko a cikin app: Saituna → Sarrafa Biyan kuɗi

5. Riƙe bayanai da Sharewa

5.1 Riƙe bayanai

  • Ana adana bayanan lafiya akan na'urarkahar abadahar sai kun goge shi da hannu
  • Ana adana bayanan motsa jiki don samar da bin diddigin ayyukan tarihi da nazari

5.2 Share Data

Kuna iya share bayananku a kowane lokaci:

Hanyar 1: Goge Ayyukan Jiki ɗaya

  • Bude allo dalla-dalla na motsa jiki
  • Matsa maɓallin sharewa ( icon ɗin shara)
  • Tabbatar da gogewa

Hanyar 2: Share All App Data

  • iOS:Share kuma sake shigar da app (an cire duk bayanan gida)
  • Android:Saituna → Apps → Run Analytics → Adana → Share bayanai

Hanyar 3: Uninstall da App

  • Cire Run Analytics yana share duk bayanan gida ta atomatik

Hanyar 4: Shake Izinin Lafiya

  • iOS:Saituna → Keɓantawa & Tsaro → Lafiya → Run Analytics → Kashe Duk Categories
  • Android:Saituna → Apps → Haɗin Lafiya → Run Analytics → Dare duk izini

6. Tsaron Bayanai

Muna ɗaukar tsaron bayanai da mahimmanci, kodayake duk bayanan suna kan na'urarka:

6.1 Matakan Tsaro

  • Tsaro iOS:Duk bayanan da aka adana ta amfani da iOS Core Data ana kiyaye su ta iOS Keychain kuma boye-boye na na'ura. Ana kiyaye bayanai lokacin da na'urar ke kulle.
  • Tsaron Android:Duk bayanan da aka adana a cikin Database Room ana kiyaye su ta Android's ginanniyar tsaro da akwatin sandbox na app.
  • Babu isar da hanyar sadarwa:Bayanan lafiya ba zai taba barin na'urarka ba, yana kawar da shi watsa tsaro kasada
  • App Sandboxing:iOS da Akwatunan Sandar app na Android suna hana wasu apps shiga Run Bayanan bincike
  • Amintaccen Ma'aji:Ba za a iya samun damar bayanan lafiya ba tare da tantance na'urar ba (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID, yatsa, buɗe fuska)

6.2 Nauyinku

Don kare bayanan ku:

  • Ajiye na'urarka a kulle tare da lambar wucewa mai ƙarfi/biometric
  • Ci gaba da sabunta OS ɗin ku tare da sabbin facin tsaro
  • iOS:Kada ku karya na'urarku
  • Android:Kar a yi tushen na'urarka

7. Rarraba Data da Bangare Na Uku

Run Analytics baya raba bayanan lafiyar ku tare da kowane ɓangare na uku.

7.1 Babu Share Data

  • Ba ma sayar da bayanan ku
  • Ba ma raba bayanan ku tare da masu talla
  • Ba mu bayar da bayanan ku ga kamfanonin nazari ba
  • Ba mu haɗa kai da dandamali na kafofin watsa labarun ba

7.2 CSV Fitarwa (Mai Amfani kawai)

Hanya guda data bar na'urarka shine lokacinKAa bayyane:

  1. Kewaya zuwa Saituna → Raw Data Export
  2. Ƙirƙiri fayil na CSV
  3. Zaɓi don raba fayil ɗin CSV ta hanyar menu na rabon na'urar ku (imel, ajiyar girgije, aikace-aikacen aika saƙo)

Wannan gaba ɗaya yana ƙarƙashin ikon ku.

10. Haƙƙinku (GDPR, CCPA Compliance)

Kodayake Run Analytics baya tattara bayanan sirri akan sabar, muna mutunta haƙƙoƙin sirrin bayanan ku:

10.1 Haƙƙin GDPR (Masu Amfani da Turai)

  • Haƙƙin shiga:Ana samun damar duk bayanan ku a cikin app a kowane lokaci
  • Haƙƙin Sharewa:Share bayanai ta amfani da hanyoyin da aka bayyana a Sashe na 5.2
  • Haƙƙin ɗaukakawa:Fitar da bayanan ku zuwa tsarin CSV (Saituna → Fitar da Raw Data)
  • Haƙƙin Ƙuntata Gudanarwa:Soke izinin lafiya don dakatar da sabon damar bayanai

10.2 Haƙƙin CCPA (Masu Amfani da California)

  • Haƙƙin Sani:Wannan manufar tana bayyana duk bayanan da aka samu da kuma yadda ake amfani da su
  • Haƙƙin Sharewa:Share bayanai ta amfani da hanyoyin da aka bayyana a Sashe na 5.2
  • Haƙƙin ficewa daga Siyarwa:Ba a zartar (ba mu taɓa sayar da bayanai ba)
